02 / SELECTED MILESTONES

Different moments. Different lessons.

◇ CLASS 6 • ACADEMIC SELECTION

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya

I was selected for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya in Class 6 and studied there for one year. It remains one of the earliest significant academic milestones of my school journey.

✎ CREATIVE WRITING

Story & Essay Writing

I won school-level competitions in both story writing and essay writing. These experiences strengthened my interest in language, creativity and expression.

02 TWO CONSECUTIVE YEARS

Literary Prefect

I served as Literary Prefect for two consecutive years. The role allowed me to develop responsibility, leadership, communication and participation in literary activities.

◉ PUBLIC SPEAKING • STAGE

Best Anchor Award

Receiving the Best Anchor Award became an important recognition of my communication skills, stage presence and confidence while speaking in front of an audience.

✦ CLASS 12 • ACADEMIC RECOGNITION

Felicitated for Board Performance

My Class 12 board performance brought one of the most memorable academic moments of my school years when I was felicitated by an ISRO scientist.
